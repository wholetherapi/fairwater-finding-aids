## Collection history

Ninety-four copperplate engravings of Marsh Lock and the surrounding navigation
works, drawn between 1876 and 1883 for the river commissioners. The plates were
transferred from the engineer's office in 1952.

## Arrangement

Engravings are numbered in the order recorded in the commissioners' minute book.
Provenance notes track each plate from the engineer's office to the archive.

## Access

Open access. Plate images are served as access derivatives; preservation files
remain on the archive store.
