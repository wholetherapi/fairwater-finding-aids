## Collection history

The Pellow River Postcards were produced by the Pellow Printing Company between
1902 and 1938 and posted along the river trade route. The collection was donated
by the Harrow family in 1968 and digitised from the original postcards in 2025.

## Arrangement

Cards are arranged by publisher series, then by postmark date. Box-level
inventory records sit alongside item-level descriptive metadata; postmark dates
are given only when legible.

## Access

Open access. Researchers should cite the item identifier and the postmark date
where known.
