## Collection history

Thirty-eight cipher notebooks kept by the Quill circle between 1931 and 1947.
The notebooks were seized during a customs inspection and transferred to the
archive in 1961; the cipher itself has never been fully solved.

## Arrangement

Notebooks are arranged by seizure bundle. Descriptive work is incomplete and
several leaves are too fragile to handle.

## Access

Access is restricted while a rights review is in progress. Written permission
from the estate is required for consultation.
