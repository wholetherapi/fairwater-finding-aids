## Collection history

Two hundred and eleven property deeds for the Brantley estate, dating from 1712
to 1889. The deeds were deposited on loan in 1936 and converted to a permanent
gift in 2004.

## Arrangement

Deeds are arranged by parish and then by conveyance date. Manorial court copies
are listed with the original indentures.

## Access

Open access. Some parchment items require supervised handling; request them at
least two working days in advance.
