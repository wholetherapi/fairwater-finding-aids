## Collection history

The survey was commissioned by the Harlow District Council to document the
valley's water-powered industry before the new reservoir flooded the lower
reach. Photographer Elihu Marsh worked across seventeen mill sites between
1894 and 1911, exposing 1,842 plates.

## Arrangement

Negatives are arranged by site and then by plate number. The original glass
plates live in the archive's climate-controlled vault; this finding aid
describes the digitisation programme only.

## Access

Reading-room access is open. Copy requests are handled through the reference
desk using the item identifiers in the digitisation manifests.
